Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) Notice

Apex Digital SA (Pty) Ltd (Registration No: 2026/237102/07) is committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you submit a website intake form, request a custom demo, or interact with our digital tools. This includes:

  • Full Name and Company Details
  • Direct Email Address and WhatsApp / Telephone Contact Number
  • Project Requirements, Budget Allocations, and Technical Specifications
  • Technical Server Logs (IP address, browser type, referrer headers for security telemetry)

2. Purpose & Lawful Basis of Processing

Your personal data is collected under Section 11(1)(a)-(b) of POPIA for the explicit purposes of:

  • Evaluating technical requirements and preparing custom project proposals or statements of work (SOW).
  • Scheduling 1-on-1 strategy consultations with our Lead Systems Architect.
  • Delivering bespoke custom code engineering, web application hosting, and post-launch maintenance.

3. Data Security & Third-Party Transfers

All data submitted through our platforms is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS encryption. Apex Digital SA does not sell, rent, or monetize client or prospect data. Data is shared strictly with infrastructure providers essential to service delivery (e.g., encrypted form dispatch handlers and cloud hosting nodes).

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Sections 23 & 24 of POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal information.
  • Request correction, destruction, or deletion of your personal records.
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds.

To exercise your rights, contact our Information Officer at apexdigtl@gmail.com.
